# Lichtreaktor
It's a small, round arc reactor lookalike that produces light in cool patterns. Press the rotary encoder to switch patterns, and turn it to control the brightness.


## What will I need to build this?

- Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040 (same one I used for my super cool and sick Hackpad)
- 24 blue LEDs (WP7113QBD/D) (ChatGPT's favourites)
- 24 resistors (220Ω) 1/4w
- A PCB (self-designed ofc)
- 6 transistors
- Rotary encoder with push button
